# ADR-014: Fail-Fast Synchronous Payment Retries (<500ms Ceiling)

- **ID:** ADR-014
- **Status:** active
- **Decided At:** 1757580800
- **Governs Service:** `payments-api`
- **Approved By:** `@sarah-staff-eng`, `@priya-sre-oncall`

## Context
During `INC-99`, a 5000ms synchronous retry backoff in `payments/retry_policy.py` caused upstream callers (`cart-service` and `checkout-web`) to exhaust their HTTP/gRPC worker pools within 12 seconds.

## Decision Rule
Tier-1 synchronous payment paths must fail fast under 500ms to prevent checkout thread-pool exhaustion. Any retry >500ms must be offloaded to async Kafka (`topic://payments.events.v1`).
